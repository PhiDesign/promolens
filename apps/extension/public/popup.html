PromoLens

Adds one small button in the header of an opened post. Click it to analyse that post. Nothing is analysed until you click.

Clicking the button on a feed card fetches that post in the background (body, links, top comments) and scores it, so you can decide whether to open it. One post per click, never automatically.

Log in with your Reddit account so posts and profiles are read through Reddit's Data API (read-only) instead of the page session. Nothing is posted or changed on your account.

When you click the button, PromoLens also reads the author's recent public posts and comments (the same pages you could open yourself) to see whether the same product recurs. Kept in memory only, for up to 6 hours per author; never saved to disk.

Optional. When you click the button, the post's text, comments and the author's history summary are also sent to a language model, which adds quoted evidence. The rule engine still computes the score. Off by default.

Free: 25 analyses to start, then 5 a month. PromoLens Plus: 500 a month.

Upgrade to Plus - $4.99 / month

Stored only in this extension on this device and sent only to OpenAI. Usage is billed to your OpenAI account - roughly half a cent per post with gpt-5-mini. Create a key at platform.openai.com/api-keys.

Estimates are based on observable signals and can be wrong. Not affiliated with Reddit.
