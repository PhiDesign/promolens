PromoLens

Adds one small button in the header of an opened post. Click it to analyse that post. Nothing is analysed until you click.

Clicking the button on a feed card fetches that post in the background (body, links, top comments) and scores it, so you can decide whether to open it. One post per click, never automatically.

When you click the button, PromoLens also reads the author's recent public posts and comments (the same pages you could open yourself) to see whether the same product recurs. Kept in memory only, for up to 6 hours per author; never saved to disk.

Optional. When you click the button, the post's visible text and comments are also sent to the API you run on your own machine, where a language model adds quoted evidence. Off by default.

Estimates are based on observable signals and can be wrong. Not affiliated with Reddit.
