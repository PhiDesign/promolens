You stay in control

Nothing happens until you click.

PromoLens analyses only the post you choose. It combines transparent local rules with a structured contextual AI review, then explains the result in plain language.

Choose a post

A small button appears on Reddit post pages and, if enabled, feed cards. There is no automatic scanning.

Run the analysis

PromoLens checks wording, links, disclosure, visible comments and the author's recent public activity.

Inspect the evidence

See promotional likelihood, disclosure status, confidence and the specific reasons that influenced the estimate.

Designed for nuance

Promotion is not automatically bad.

PromoLens distinguishes transparent creators from posts where a commercial connection may be missing or unclear.

01

Evidence, not accusations

Every score is an estimate based on observable signals. PromoLens never reports, hides or publicly labels anyone.

02

Disclosure-aware

“I built this” is treated differently from a product recommendation that leaves the author's connection unstated.

03

Privacy-minded

No accounts, advertising or data selling. Post text is processed only after your click and is not retained by PromoLens.
