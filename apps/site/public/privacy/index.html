Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · Operated under the Amadoso brand

1. Summary

PromoLens does nothing until you click its button on a Reddit post. That click processes the selected post, its visible comments and a compact summary of the author's recent public Reddit activity. Nothing is sent for posts you do not choose. PromoLens does not sell personal data, run advertising or train models on Reddit content.

2. Information we process

For the post you select, PromoLens may process its identifier, permalink, title, visible body, author name, subreddit, age, votes, comments, outbound links, disclosure wording and Reddit's visible labels. It may also read recent public posts and comments from that author's public profile to detect recurring products or prior ownership disclosures.

The extension creates a random anonymous installation identifier. The hosted service uses it to count monthly analyses and, for Plus, associate the Paddle transaction and subscription identifiers you activate. It is not intentionally linked to your name, Reddit account or browsing history.

We receive ordinary technical request information such as IP address and user agent through hosting infrastructure. We do not use it to build profiles or track browsing activity.

3. How information is used

Selected content is used only to produce the requested analysis, prevent abuse, enforce plan limits and operate the Service. The rule engine computes the final score. A structured contextual review contributes bounded evidence and explanatory signals.

4. Service providers

  • Cloudflare hosts this website and the PromoLens service and may process network and security logs.
  • TypeSafe System One provides the structured contextual AI review for the post you choose.
  • Paddle is our Merchant of Record for PromoLens Plus and processes payment details, billing contact information, taxes and invoices. PromoLens never receives your complete card information.
  • Reddit provides the public content being analysed. Your use of Reddit remains subject to Reddit's own terms and privacy practices.

5. Retention

PromoLens does not retain raw post or comment text after the requested analysis. Results are cached in the extension under a content hash for up to 24 hours. The service retains the anonymous installation identifier and monthly usage count; inactive free counters are removed after two months. Plus records retain transaction and subscription identifiers as needed to provide access, prevent redemption abuse and meet legal obligations.

6. What PromoLens does not access

PromoLens does not access private messages, chats, saved items, other websites, general browser history or pages you have not chosen to analyse. It never posts, votes, reports or changes content on your behalf.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can hide PromoLens buttons, disable feed-card access, clear cached results, remove Plus from an installation, disable the extension or uninstall it. To request deletion of hosted usage or subscription bindings, contact us through the support link below and provide the anonymous installation identifier shown by the extension when requested.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online system is completely secure. Providers may process information in countries other than yours under their applicable safeguards and legal obligations.

9. Children

PromoLens is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the Service changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice where required.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Amadoso through the PromoLens support tracker.
